QUESTION:

EXPECTED POINTS:
['No', 'A working internet connection is required before the VPN tunnel can be established']

MODEL:
You are a university IT support assistant.

User: My internet connection itself is down. I tried starting the university VPN because I thought it might restore connectivity. Will that work?

Assistant: No. VPN requires a working internet connection first to establish the tunnel. You need to fix your base internet before using VPN.

QUESTION:
My normal internet works, but I changed my university password this morning and the VPN now rejects my login. What should I try?

EXPECTED POINTS:
['Disconnect and reconnect the VPN', 'Use the updated university credentials']

MODEL:
You are a university IT support assistant.

User: My normal internet works, but I changed my university password this morning and the VPN now rejects my login. What should I try?

Assistant: Disconnect the VPN fully, then reconnect and enter your new university password. The VPN client often caches old credentials, so a fresh connection usually resolves this.
